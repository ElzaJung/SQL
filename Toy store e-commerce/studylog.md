## Business Task
Company: Maven Fuzzy Factory - a fast-growing, D2C e-commerce retailer selling plush toys and children's gifts.  
Company Stage: Scaling phase.  

### Stakeholders
1. Executive Leadership: Revenue report.  
2. UI/UX Team: User funnel analysis.  
3. Marketing Team: Marketing channel performance.

Dataset Source: [Maven Analytics Toy Store Database](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/toy-store-e-commerce-databasea)

### Q2. Are visitors bouncing off our custom landing pages?
<img width="781" height="454" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7485dc69-b878-40f6-aaff-9698a1cc69f8" />

#### Steps
1. Identify distinct `pageview_url` values (`/home`, `/lander-1` to `/lander-5`).  
2. Aggregate session IDs and landing pages using the `ARRAY_AGG` function.  
3. Flag bounced sessions where `total_page_view` equals 1.  
4. Calculate bounce rate as `bounced_sessions / total_sessions`.  

#### Results
<img width="641" height="195" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8817843-adb9-4441-8508-dd96a844b4f9" />

#### Recommendation
`/lander-1`, `/lander-3`, and `/lander-4` have bounce rates higher than 50%. Recommend UI/UX team redesign these pages and run A/B testing.

### Q3. Which marketing channels have been most successful?
<img width="745" height="479" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/454ba25a-9ca7-4a6e-8c02-2ac6c2312873" />

#### Steps
1. Extract `utm_source` parameters from traffic URLs.  
2. Label `NULL` sources as `direct`.  
3. Aggregate total sessions and total revenue.  
4. Group data by `utm_source`, `utm_content`, and `device_type`.  
5. Sort results by `total_revenue_usd` descending.

#### Analysis
1. Desktop generates higher Revenue Per Session ($4.46 - $6.36) than mobile ($0.57 - $1.93) across all networks.  
2. `ad_2` outperforms `ad_1` on both gsearch ($6.36 vs $4.90) and bsearch ($6.06 vs $4.46) on desktop.  
3. Socialbook ads perform poorly, especially `social_ad_1` on mobile ($0.57 revenue/session).  
4. Non-paid direct desktop traffic yields $6.01 revenue/session.  

#### Recommendation
Shift ad spend from mobile to desktop and reallocate budget from `ad_1` to `ad_2`. Pause `social_ad_1` campaigns and invest in SEO and email retention.
